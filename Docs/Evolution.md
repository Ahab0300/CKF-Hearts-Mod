# Evolution of Relationships
## Basic Relationships

Basic Relationships can change into other Basic Relationships through
[Heart Events](HeartEvents.md), but usually only along certain pathways:

- New relationship can open usually with Degree=1 (if no prior relationship)
- Change Degree up or down by 1
- Change Category but not Degree
- In rare cases, can change Polarity but not Category or Degree

So, a BestFriend relationship could decline to Friend, become exaggerated into Follows,
or perhaps evolve into Attracted, Respects, Supports, or Beholden. In a bad circumstance,
BestFriend could become Rival.

Basic Relationships can be one-sided or unbalanced. One partner might consider another
to be a Friend while the other considers the relationship BestFriend. A Degree 3
Relationship is usually unbalanced. Degree 3 Relationships are usually somewhat extreme,
with the partner having a lower Degree return relationship, possibly even in a different
category.

## Sexual Relationships

Sexual Relationships can only change into other Sexual Relationships. Once you
gain a Sexual Relationship you will always have a Sexual Relationship but the
nature of the relationship can change. A breakup with a Lover could turn into ExLover or
Jilted, depending on the [Heart Events](HeartEvents.md). Gaining a Spouse (Marriage) could
be proposed with a Lover or with someone you were Attracted to. A Spouse could become a
SpouseBad, an ExSpouse, or an ExSpouseGood.

A Sexual Relationship is mutual. Each partner will be a Lover, a Spouse, an ExLover, etc.
If a Lover relationship becomes Jilted, both get the Jilted relationship - the tag does
not specify which partner jilted the other. The only exception is if one partner goes away.

If a sexual partner (good or bad) dies or leaves the team, the remaining partner will
change their relationship into Misses. The former relationship will be remembered in a
hidden tag. The Misses relationship may result in new [Heart Events](HeartEvents.md)
due to their grief. Eventually the relationship might disappear. Time heals all wounds.
The hidden relationship will still exist, recording the previous relationship.

If the former Sexual partner reappears, there will fairly quickly be a new event in
which the two try to figure out their new relationship. It can return to the previous
state or they can forge a new relationship. The hidden relationship will be removed.

A change in a Sexual Relationship can trigger changes in the Relationships they have
with other people.
