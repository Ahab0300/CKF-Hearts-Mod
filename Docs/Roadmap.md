# Roadmap

This is a general roadmap of features we would like to incorporate into the **Hearts Mod**

- Interface to configure the **Hearts Mod**
- Heart Event to create a relationship between two mercs who do not have one
- Heart Events to potentially change relationships
- Casual chats between mercs who have a relationship
- Expand quips to reduce repetition
- Add quips activated by Tags
- Add Heart Stories to explore consequences of relationships
