[Cyber Knights:Flashpoint](https://cyberknightswiki.tresebrothers.com/), or `CKF`, is a turn-based squad tactics RPG avalable on
[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1021210/Cyber_Knights_Flashpoint/).
Players lead a squad of hackers, mercs, and malcontents on their choice of heists,
navigating the power-player politics of 23rd-century cyberpunk New Boston to build
their rep and access ever-riskier and more rewarding missions.

This **Hearts Mod** expands the content of `Cyber Knights:Flashpoint` by introducing evolving interpersonal relationships which
impact personnel management in the game. Events based on Hearts relationships can cause Stress and Hype, affect merc
availability, and lead to unpredicted interactions.

[Full Hearts Mod Documentation](Docs/Main.md)

The **Hearts Mod** currently adds the following material to the base game:

- 21 new Relationship Tags to flesh out the existing Relationship Tags

Features currently in development:

- Interface to configure the **Hearts Mod**
- Interaction to create a relationship between two mercs who do not have one
- Interactions to potentially change relationships
- Casual chats between mercs who have a relationship
