# Chatter
A Chatter is a casual dialog that may occur between two characters who have a relationship. The Chatter
will have no consequences. A Chatter event will appear on the character profile of the character designated
by the Casting Director as the Actor. The player may open the dialog or may let it expire without repercussion.
